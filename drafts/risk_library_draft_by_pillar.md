## Top cross-cutting themes (S&BD lens)

1. **Rate uplift vs. competitive / utilization reality** — organic plan depends on renewals resetting toward newbuild-parity economics; soft segment utilization or tender pressure can defer that.
2. **Industry orderbook and capacity timing** — temporary oversupply in some vessel classes can dilute utilization before longer-term demand intensity catches up.
3. **Geographic mix (esp. Norway share build)** — platform has strong positions in selected regions and an emerging Norway ambition; execution and award risk is material to growth.
4. **Investment underwriting quality** — newbuild / reconstruction / M&A must clear yield–IRR hurdles amid CAPEX inflation, yard slots, and charter uncertainty.
5. **Contract coverage, customer quality, and concentration** — multiple expansion rests on TC tenure, counterparty strength, and diversification of farmer exposure.
6. **Fleet quality, obsolescence, and disciplined divestment** — technology, size, and fish-handling standards drive residual value and commercial relevance.
7. **Cash conversion through the investment cycle** — progress payments, maintenance capex, DSO, and cash interest can disconnect FCF from EBITDA.
8. **Leverage, liquidity, and capital allocation trade-offs** — growth vs. delever vs. distributions needs binding rules to protect exit readiness.
9. **Regulatory / farming-technology shift** — closed, land-based, offshore, or regional policy changes can reshape vessel-demand mix over a longer horizon.
10. **Governance, scalability, and key-person risk** — institutional quality and bandwidth affect both execution and exit multiple.

Operational HSEQ detail from the IM is intentionally **not** duplicated here; link those risks in Technical / Vessel Operations registers where needed.
